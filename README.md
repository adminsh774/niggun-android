## What is Niggun?

Niggun is a clean, ad-free player for Jewish music: Chassidish, Yeshivish, Israeli, kumzitz, a cappella and kids' music. Every song is checked against a trusted catalog before it appears, so what you hear is music you can play at home.

This is the native Android app. It looks exactly like the web app at [play.sh774.uk](https://play.sh774.uk), with a real Android music player underneath.

## Features

- 🎵 **Native player** with lock-screen and notification controls
- 🎧 **Bluetooth and headset buttons**: play, pause, next, previous
- 🔥 **Trending, new albums and 2,000+ artists**
- 🔀 **Shuffle, repeat and autoplay** that keeps the music going
- 🛡️ **Kol Isha filter** to hide women's and girls' vocals
- 🌙 **Dark mode**: Automatic, Dark or Light
- ⏰ **Sleep timer**
- ❤️ **Optional account** to keep loved songs, playlists and followed artists in sync across devices

## Install

1. On your Android phone, open the [latest release](https://github.com/adminsh774/niggun-android/releases/latest).
2. Tap **Niggun-1.0.0.apk** to download it.
3. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow installing apps from this source.
4. Tap **Install**, then open Niggun.

> Requires Android 7.0 or newer. On iPhone, open [play.sh774.uk](https://play.sh774.uk) in Safari and tap Share → Add to Home Screen.

## Updating

New versions are posted on the [Releases](https://github.com/adminsh774/niggun-android/releases) page. Download the new APK and install it over the old one; your settings stay.

## Privacy

No ads and no tracking. An account is optional and only stores your own library.
